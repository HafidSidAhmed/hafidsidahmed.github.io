## Course Overview

This practical course introduced first-year Mathematics and Computer Science students to scientific computing through the implementation of numerical methods.

The teaching focused on the practical implementation of numerical methods related to four main topics:

- Functions, limits, and continuity
- Numerical differentiation
- Numerical integration and antiderivatives
- Numerical methods for partial differential equations

The practical sessions used Scilab, a free scientific computing software environment similar to MATLAB.

The objective was to translate mathematical methods into computational algorithms and implement them to solve numerical problems.

## Prerequisites

- High-school level mathematics
- Basic knowledge of functions, derivatives, and integrals
- Familiarity with mathematical notation

- Two student groups
