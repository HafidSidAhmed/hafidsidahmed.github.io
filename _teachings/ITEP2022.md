## Course Overview

This teaching experience involved providing mathematics instruction to children and adolescents aged 5 to 15 in a specialised educational setting.

The teaching was adapted to the students' different ages, educational levels, and individual learning needs.

Classes were deliberately kept small, with **2 to 6 students per group**, allowing for close individual support and differentiated teaching.

## Teaching Format

-Children and adolescents aged 5 to 15
-Small groups of 2 to 6 students
-35 teaching hours per week
-One-month full-time teaching period
-Differentiated mathematics instruction
-Language: French

## Teaching Approach

The teaching focused on adapting mathematical explanations and exercises to each student's level and learning needs, with an emphasis on individual support and progressive consolidation of fundamental concepts.
