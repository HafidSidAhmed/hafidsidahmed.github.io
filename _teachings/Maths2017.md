## Course Overview

This course provided mathematics support to students experiencing difficulties in order to help them prepare for the French vocational certificate examination (Brevet Professionnel).

The teaching focused on consolidating fundamental mathematical concepts, addressing individual difficulties, and preparing students for the mathematical requirements of the examination.

## Teaching Format

- Small-group or individual support
- 1 hour per week
- Targeted support for students experiencing difficulties
- Examination preparation
- Language: French

## Assessment

The sessions focused on guided exercises, consolidation of fundamental concepts, and preparation for the Brevet Professionnel examination.
